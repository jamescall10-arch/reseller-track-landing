Features

Everything a reseller actually needs

📦

Inventory management

Organise your stock into categories, track what you paid, and move items to active listings with one click. Always know what you have.

🏷️

Active listings tracker

See everything currently live on eBay in one place. Search sold prices instantly, log a sale, or pull something back to stock.

Bundle buy calculator

Enter the values of items in a listing one by one. See the total after eBay fees and postage, then get a clear "maximum you should pay" figure.

Sales & profit tracking

Log each sale with the actual eBay fees charged. See your real money in, postage paid, and true profit on every transaction.

Smart fee calculator

How it works

Up and running in minutes

1

Add your stock

Create categories for what you sell and add your items with their values and what you paid. Your inventory is instantly organised.

2

List on eBay

When you list an item, move it to Active Listings with one click. Search eBay sold prices directly from the app to set the right price.

3

Log your sales

When something sells, enter the sale price and postage. ResellerTrack calculates your real profit after all fees automatically.
